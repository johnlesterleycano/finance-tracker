## 1. The idea in one minute

You get paid twice a month, on the 15th and the 30th. The app gives **each payday its own plan**:

```
  Income on this payday
+ Leftover carried in from the last payday
− Bills this payday pays
− Money moved into savings funds
− Spending money (groceries, gas, eating out)
= Left to assign
```

The goal is to get **Left to assign** close to zero: every peso has a job. If some is left over, it carries into the next payday automatically.

You set things up once (your income, your monthly bills, your savings funds). After that, each new payday plan is filled in for you, and your job is to tick bills as you pay them.

## 2. Words you'll see

| Word | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Payday plan** | One payday's budget: the 15th or the 30th of one month. |
| **Recurring bill** | A bill you pay every month (rent, a card, internet). Set once, copied into every new plan. |
| **Income source** | Someone's salary or another regular income, with its usual amount on the 15th and on the 30th. |
| **Savings fund** | A place you put money aside: an emergency fund, a fund for a yearly cost, or any goal. |
| **Spending money** | A budget for day-to-day spending until the next payday. You log what you spend against it. |
| **Left to assign** (or **Unallocated cash**) | Money in this plan that has no job yet. |
| **Carried in** | Leftover money from the previous payday that rolls into this one. |
| **Bills load** | Bills ÷ income. It shows how heavy a payday is. |
| **Comfortable / Watch / Tight** | Health labels for the bills load: up to 50% is Comfortable, up to 70% is Watch, above 70% is Tight. You can change these limits. |
| **On track / Assign it / Over budget** | Health labels for Left to assign: below zero is Over budget, above 5% of income is Assign it, anything in between is On track. |

The 15th plan covers the 15th up to the day before the 30th. The 30th plan covers the 30th (the 28th or 29th in February) up to the 14th of the next month.

## 3. Opening the app for the first time

The first screen asks **"Where should your plan live?"** Pick one:

**Connect my Sheet:** your plan is saved in your own private Google Sheet, so the same plan shows up on your computer and on your phone, and two people can share it.
1. Paste the **Google Sheet web app link** (it starts with `https://script.google.com/macros/s/` and ends in `/exec`).
2. Type the **passcode** that was set for that Sheet.
3. Select **Connect my Sheet**.

Don't have a Sheet yet? Whoever set up the app follows the [setup guide](SETUP.md) first (about 15 minutes). The passcode is never saved on your device; the app keeps a private sign-in token instead.

**Use on this device only:** the plan is saved in this browser and nowhere else. It's the quickest way to try the app. You can connect a Sheet later, and your plan is uploaded to it if the Sheet is still empty.

> Saving on this device only means clearing your browser data erases your plan. Export a backup now and then (see [section 11](#11-your-data-saving-syncing-and-backups)).

**Install it like an app (optional, on your phone):**
- iPhone (Safari): Share › **Add to Home Screen**.
- Android (Chrome): menu › **Install app**.

## 4. Your first plan (about 10 minutes)

When nothing is set up yet, the Payday screen shows **"Let's set up your money plan"** with four steps. Each step gets a check mark when it's done.

### Step 1: Your income on each payday

Two sources are there to start with, **Income 1** and **Income 2**. Select one to edit it:
- **Name:** whose income it is, for example a person's name or "Freelance".
- **Usual on 15th** and **Usual on 30th:** what normally arrives on each payday. Use the amount that actually reaches your account (after tax and deductions).

Use **Add income source** for more, and open a source and select **Delete** (twice, to confirm) to remove one you don't need.

*Example:* "Income 1" gets ₱20,000 on the 15th and ₱20,000 on the 30th.

### Step 2: Bills you pay every month

Select **Add a recurring bill** for each bill:

| Field | What to put |
| --- | --- |
| **Bill** | Its name, for example "Rent" or "Credit card". |
| **Usual amount** | What you normally pay. You can change the amount in any single plan later. |
| **Category** | Groups bills in charts. The defaults are Cards & Loans, Family & Household, Utilities and Other. |
| **Paid from** | **15th**, **30th**, or **Both paydays** (the full amount on each payday, for example a weekly allowance split in two). |
| **Due day of the month** | Optional. With a due day, the plan shows the due date and warns you three days before. |
| **Last month** | Optional. The last month this bill appears, for example when a loan's last installment is in March. |
| **Include in new plans** | Untick to pause a bill without deleting it. |

Tip: pick the payday that comes **before** the due date. A bill due on the 5th is usually paid from the 30th payday.

### Step 3: Savings funds (optional)

Select **Add a fund**. See [Savings funds](#savings-funds) for the three types. Put the amounts you want to move on the 15th and on the 30th; every new plan includes them as savings to move.

### Step 4: Create your first payday plan

Select **Create my first plan**. The app builds the plan for the current payday from your income, recurring bills, funds and spending money. You'll land on the Payday screen.

You can always change setup later under **Trends & setup**.

### The tour

The first time a payday plan is on screen, a short tour (7 steps, about a minute) points out the parts you'll use most. You can use the app while it's open: try ticking a bill. Select **Skip tour** or press **Esc** to close it; on a computer, **←** and **→** step through it. Replay it any time with **Take the tour** in Trends & setup.

## 5. Getting around

The app has four sections:

| Section | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Payday** | Day-to-day: one payday's income, bills, savings and spending. You'll spend most of your time here. |
| **Month** | Both paydays of a month side by side, health checks and charts. |
| **Funds & loans** | Savings fund balances and targets, loans, and the debt payoff planner. |
| **Trends & setup** | The last 12 months, plus all setup: recurring bills, income, yearly extras, rules and your data. |

**On a computer or laptop** (window 900px wide or more):
- The sections are in the **sidebar** on the left.
- The header shows the month, the **15th payday / 30th payday** switch (on the Payday screen), and the **‹ ›** buttons to change month.
- Keyboard: **←** and **→** change month, **1** to **4** switch section. Shortcuts are off while you're typing or while an edit window is open.
- Each screen is a statement on the left with a summary panel on the right (below it on narrower windows).
- Bill actions (Paid, Part paid, Edit) appear when you hover over a bill or move to it with Tab.
- Edit windows open in the middle of the screen. Close them with **Esc**, the **✕**, or by clicking outside.

**On a phone:**
- The sections are in the **tab bar** at the bottom.
- **Swipe a bill left** to see Paid, Part paid and Edit.
- Edit windows slide up from the bottom. Drag the handle down, tap **✕**, or tap outside to close.

**Everywhere:**
- Select almost any row (a bill, an income, a fund, an expense) to open it and edit it.
- **Delete** buttons ask twice: the first select changes the label to "Select again to delete", the second deletes.
- The status chip (**Synced to your Sheet**, **Saved on this device**, **Offline**…) shows where your changes are. See [section 11](#11-your-data-saving-syncing-and-backups).

## 6. Your payday routine

What to do each time you get paid. It takes a few minutes.

1. **Open the payday.** The app opens on the payday that still has unpaid bills. If the plan doesn't exist yet, select **Create 15th plan** (or 30th).
2. **Check the income.** If an amount was different this time (overtime, a deduction), select it and change it.
3. **Pay your bills and tick them.** Select the round check box next to each bill you pay. If you paid only part, use **Part paid** and type how much.
4. **Move your savings.** When you transfer money to a fund, tick it under **Savings plan**. Only ticked transfers count in the fund's balance.
5. **Look at Left to assign.** If it says **Assign it**, give the extra a job: add it to a fund, or leave it to carry into the next payday. If it says **Over budget**, lower something or move a bill to the other payday.
6. **Log spending as it happens.** Add each expense under **Spending money** until the next payday.
7. **Do a bank check (recommended).** Enter what your account holds. On the next payday the app tells you if anything is missing from the plan.

Once a month, glance at the **Month** screen to see whether one payday carries too much.

## 7. The Payday screen

The header switch picks **15th payday** or **30th payday**; **‹ ›** changes month. If a payday has no plan yet you'll see **Create 15th plan** (or 30th): it builds the plan from your setup, and you can change any amount afterwards.

On a computer the screen reads like a statement: Income, Bills, Savings plan, Spending money and Bank check run down the page, each with its total on the right. The summary sits beside it and stays in view while you scroll. On a phone the same parts are cards, with the summary on top.

### The summary

- **The big number** is **Left to assign** for this payday, with its label (On track / Assign it / Over budget).
- Below it, the whole sum: **Income**, plus anything **Carried in**, minus **Bills** (with the bills load %), **Savings** and **Spending money**, equals **Left to assign**. On a phone you see the four totals as tiles instead.

### Due soon

Under the summary, a list of unpaid bills that are overdue or due within 3 days, from any payday up to the next one. Select one to jump to its payday. Only bills with a due date appear here.

### Bills

- The chip in the corner is this payday's bills load: **Comfortable**, **Watch** or **Tight**.
- The bar shows how much of the bills you've paid so far.
- Unpaid bills are on top; paid bills move to the **Paid** list below.
- Each bill shows its category color, a due chip (**Due in 2 days**, **Due today**, **Overdue Oct 3**), and notes.

What you can do:
- **Tick the check box** to mark a bill paid (today's date is saved). Tick again to mark it unpaid.
- **Part paid** (hover on a computer, swipe left on a phone): type the amount paid so far. The check box shows a half state.
- **Select the bill** (or **Edit**) to change its name, amount, category, status, date paid, due date or notes, or to **Delete** it from this plan.
- **Add bill** adds a one-off bill to this payday. Tick **Repeat on every 15th** (or 30th) to also make it a recurring bill.

When every bill is paid you'll see a little celebration. If a loan's last installment is paid, the app tells you it's paid off.

Changing a bill here changes **this payday only**. To change it for the future, edit the recurring bill in Trends & setup.

### Spending money

A budget for everyday spending until the next payday.
- Select **Budget ₱…** to set the amount. Tick **Use this for every 15th payday** (or 30th) to make it the default for new plans.
- **Add expense:** amount, date and what it was for. Select an expense to edit or delete it.
- The big number is what's left until the next payday. It turns red if you spend more than the budget.

The budget is set aside like a bill. If you overspend, the extra comes out of Left to assign.

### Savings plan

The transfers this payday makes into your funds, filled in from each fund's 15th / 30th amount.
- **Tick** a transfer once the money has actually moved. Only moved transfers add to the fund's balance.
- **Select** a transfer to change the amount or the fund, or to delete it.
- **Add to a fund** adds another transfer. Choose **Other (type a name)** for money set aside that isn't one of your funds.
- The totals at the bottom add up savings, spending money, money carried in, and the **Unallocated cash** that's left.

When a fund reaches its target, the app celebrates.

### Income

- Select an income to change this payday's amount, for example a smaller paycheck or a bonus.
- **Add income** adds a one-off income to this payday only.
- **Pay date** is the date this payday's money arrives (the 15th, or the 30th or the last day of February). Change it if your pay came early or late.

### Bank check

It catches spending you forgot to log.
1. After your pay arrives and you've ticked what you paid, select **Enter balance** and type what the account your pay goes into holds right now.
2. The first check is your **starting point**.
3. On a later payday, enter the balance again. The app compares it with **last check + everything logged since** (income in, bills paid, savings moved, spending logged):
   - **Matches the plan**: everything adds up.
   - **₱… not in the plan**: there's less money than expected, so a bill or some spending probably wasn't logged.
   - **₱… more than planned**: there's more money than expected, so maybe income or a refund wasn't logged.

Select **Update** to change the balance, or **Clear** to remove it.

### Delete this payday plan

At the bottom of the page. Select it twice to delete this payday's plan. Your setup (recurring bills, funds, income sources) isn't touched, and you can create the plan again.

## 8. The Month screen

Both paydays of the month on screen, side by side. Use **‹ ›** to change month. If the month has no plans yet, select **Create [month]** to make both paydays at once.

### Month at a glance

A table with a 15th column, a 30th column and a Month total:
- **Income**, **Bills**, **Left after bills**
- **Savings plan**, **Spending money**, **Carried in**
- **Unallocated cash** (with its label in the corner)
- **Bills as % of income** and **Bills paid so far**

If only one payday has a plan, **Create the missing payday** adds the other.

### Quick check

Health labels for the 15th, the 30th and the whole month, plus your **savings rate** (savings ÷ income) and how much is **still to pay** this month.

### Balance my paydays

If one payday carries far more bills than the other, the app suggests up to three recurring bills to move to the other payday, and shows the bills load you'd get after each move. Select **Apply** to move one.
- It only suggests bills whose due day still fits the new payday, so nothing would be paid late.
- It changes your recurring bill, so the move applies to **plans you create from now on**, not plans you've already made.
- It needs your usual income on both paydays (Trends & setup › Income sources).

### Charts

- **Income vs bills vs savings:** the two paydays compared.
- **Where the month's income goes:** a donut by bill category, savings, spending money and unallocated cash.
- **Bills: paid vs still to pay.**

## 9. The Funds & loans screen

### All savings funds

The total of every fund's balance. A fund's balance is:

```
  Opening balance (what you typed when you set it up)
+ Every transfer you ticked as moved
− Withdrawals
```

### Savings funds

Select **+ Fund** to add one, or select a fund card to edit it. There are three types:

| Type | Use it for | Its target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Regular** | Any goal: a trip, a gadget, a down payment. | The **Target** you type (optional). |
| **Emergency** | Money for a job loss or a hospital bill. | Worked out for you: 1, 3, 6 or 12 months of your essential bills (active recurring bills outside the "Other" category). |
| **Yearly cost** | A cost that comes once a year: insurance, school fees, registration. | The **Yearly cost**, due in the month you pick. The app works out how much to set aside each payday to have it in time; select **Use this amount on both paydays** to fill it in. |

For every fund:
- **On the 15th / On the 30th:** how much to move on each payday. New plans include these as savings transfers.
- **Current balance:** set it to what the account holds today. Ticked transfers add to it from then on.
- **Withdraw / For:** take money out (for example ₱3,000 "Car repair"). The last few withdrawals are listed.

The card shows the balance, a progress bar towards the target, and the regular amounts. If you have bills but no emergency fund, a tip offers to start one.

### Loans & installments

Select **+ Loan** for each loan, installment plan or credit card:

| Field | What to put |
| --- | --- |
| **Loan or card name** | For example "Phone installment". |
| **Monthly payment** | What you pay each month. |
| **Total months** | The number of installments. Use 0 for an open-ended debt like a credit card. |
| **Balance left today** | What you still owe. Needed for the payoff planner. |
| **Interest rate (% a year)** | A monthly rate of 2% is 24% a year. |
| **Bill names that pay it** | The name of the bill (or bills) that pays this loan, comma separated, exactly as it appears in your plans. |
| **Installments paid before these records** | Payments made before you started using the app. |

Each **paid bill with a matching name** counts as one installment. The ring fills up as you pay, and the card shows the months left, about how much is still to go, and when it ends. When no installments are left, the bill stops appearing in new plans.

### Debt payoff plan

For loans with a balance and a monthly payment, the planner shows your **debt-free date** and the **total interest** you'll pay, with a chart. Switch between two methods:
- **Avalanche:** pays the highest interest rate first. The least interest overall.
- **Snowball:** clears the smallest balance first. Quick wins that keep you going.

In both, you keep paying every minimum, and when one debt is gone its payment rolls into the next. The note tells you which method saves more. If the payments are too low to ever cover the interest, it says so.

## 10. The Trends & setup screen

### Last 12 months

Two charts for the 12 months up to the month on screen:
- **Income and bills:** each month's income bar next to its bills bar. On a computer, hover over a bar to see the amount.
- **Bills as a share of income:** your bills load month by month. The green band is Comfortable, the amber band is Watch, and above that is Tight.

### Recurring bills

All your recurring bills, grouped by **15th payday**, **30th payday** and **Both paydays**. Select **+ Bill** to add one, or select a bill to edit or delete it (fields are explained in [Step 2](#step-2-bills-you-pay-every-month)).

Each bill shows what it costs **a year** (the next 12 months, or until its end month), and the card shows the total. A small amount every payday can be a big number over a year: a good place to look for something to cancel.

New payday plans copy these. Changing one here **doesn't change plans you've already made**. To change a plan you've already made, edit the bill on the Payday screen.

### Income sources

Your usual income on each payday. Renaming a source also renames it in plans from the payday on screen onwards.

### Yearly extra income

Money that comes once a year: 13th month pay, a bonus, a tax refund. Select **+ Extra**, pick the month, the payday and the amount. It's added to that payday's plan when you create it.

### Rules

Select **Edit** to change:

| Rule | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Comfortable up to** | 50% | A bills load up to this is Comfortable. |
| **Tight above** | 70% | A bills load above this is Tight. Between the two is Watch. |
| **Alert if unallocated is above** | 5% of income | Above this, Left to assign says "Assign it". |
| **Carry leftover cash to the next payday** | On | Leftover (or shortfall) rolls into the next payday's plan. |
| **Carry over starting from payday** | Your first plan | The first payday that carries over, written like `2026-11-15` or `2026-11-30`. |
| **Spending money, 15th / 30th** | ₱0 | The default spending budget for new plans. |
| **Categories** | Cards & Loans, Family & Household, Utilities, Other | Comma separated. "Other" doesn't count as an essential for the emergency fund. |

### Your data

Where your plan is saved, and the buttons to manage it. See the next section.

### Appearance

Choose **Light**, **Dark**, or **Match device** (follows your computer or phone's own setting). The choice is saved on this device only, so each person sharing the plan can pick their own.

## 11. Your data: saving, syncing and backups

Everything saves by itself as soon as you select **Save**. There's no save button for the whole app.

### The status chip

In the sidebar on a computer, in the header on a phone:

| Chip | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Synced to your Sheet** | Everything is in your Sheet. | Nothing. Select it to sync again. |
| **Saving…** / **Syncing…** | Changes are on their way. | Wait a moment. |
| **Offline · 3 waiting** | No connection. Your changes are kept on this device and sent when you're back online. | Keep using the app as usual. |
| **Signed out · select to reconnect** | This device was signed out (for example with "Sign out all devices"). | Select it, then enter the link and passcode again. |
| **Saved on this device** | Device-only mode. | Export a backup now and then. |

### Trends & setup › Your data

**With a Google Sheet:**
- **Sync now:** fetch the latest from the Sheet, for example after the other person made changes on their phone.
- **Export backup:** download a copy of your plan as a file.
- **Sign out all devices:** every phone and computer has to enter the passcode again. Use it if a phone is lost.
- **Forget this device:** remove the plan from this browser. Your Sheet keeps everything.
- Backups: the Sheet keeps its own history. In Google Sheets, open **File › Version history** to go back to an earlier version.

**On this device only:**
- **Export backup:** download a copy of your plan. Do this regularly.
- **Restore backup:** load a backup file. It replaces what's on this device. (In Sheet mode, use the Sheet's version history instead.)
- **Connect a Google Sheet:** move to Sheet mode. If the Sheet is empty, this device's plan is uploaded to it. If the Sheet already has a plan, the Sheet's plan is shown instead.
- **Erase this device:** delete the plan from this browser for good. Export a backup first.

Keep backup files private: they hold your whole budget.

### Sharing with a partner

Connect both devices to the **same Sheet** with the same link and passcode. Changes from one device show up on the other after a sync (select the status chip, or **Sync now**).

## 12. Questions and fixes

**I changed a recurring bill but this payday didn't change.**
That's on purpose: plans you've already made keep their own amounts, so history stays accurate. Edit the bill in the payday plan too, or delete the plan and create it again (you'll lose ticks and logs in that plan).

**A bill is in the wrong payday.**
For this payday only: delete it here and add it to the other payday. For the future: edit the recurring bill and change **Paid from**, or use **Balance my paydays** on the Month screen.

**Left to assign is negative ("Over budget").**
Bills, savings and spending money add up to more than the income plus any money carried in. Lower a savings transfer or the spending budget, move a bill to the other payday, or check that the income amounts are right.

**Left to assign is high ("Assign it").**
You have money without a job. Add a transfer to a fund, or leave it: with carry-over on, it rolls into the next payday.

**My fund balance looks wrong.**
Only transfers you **ticked as moved** count. Open the fund, set **Current balance** to what the account really holds, and select **Save**. The app adjusts the opening balance to match.

**The loan countdown doesn't move.**
The bill name in the payday plan must match one of the loan's **Bill names that pay it** (capital letters and punctuation don't matter). Also check the bill is ticked **paid**, not part paid.

**A due date warning is missing.**
Add a **Due day of the month** to the recurring bill. Plans made before that won't have it; edit the bill in the plan and set its **Due date**.

**The keyboard shortcuts don't work.**
They're off while you type in a field or while an edit window is open. Press **Esc** or click an empty part of the page first.

**"That passcode didn't match" / "Too many wrong tries".**
Check the passcode with whoever set up the Sheet. After too many wrong tries, wait 15 minutes.

**"Couldn't reach the Sheet".**
Check your internet connection, and that the link is the full web app link ending in `/exec`.

**Where do I change the passcode?**
In the Sheet's Apps Script settings. See the [setup guide](SETUP.md#good-to-know).
